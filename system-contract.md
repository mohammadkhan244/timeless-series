**TIMELESS**

*System Contract*

Version 1.0

*This document defines the invariant structure of the Timeless Database. It is the highest-level specification in the system. All implementation, submission forms, editorial decisions, and UI design must conform to this contract. If a proposed change cannot be mapped to the core mission or the ten categories, it does not belong in Timeless.*

# **1\. WHAT TIMELESS IS**

Timeless is a human-curated database of books, films, and TV shows organized around the ten universal human crises.

It allows a person to:

* Browse a gallery of works organized by the human moment they prepare you for

* Read real explanations from real contributors about why each work lasts

* Submit a work they believe belongs, with evidence

* Receive a monthly newsletter with one book, one film, one TV show — and the best community writing on why they endure

Timeless is fundamentally about:

***Not what’s good. What lasts.***

The curatorial question is never “did this move me”. It is “would I hand this to my grandchildren and say this will help you be human.”

# **2\. WHAT TIMELESS IS NOT**

Timeless is explicitly NOT:

* A recommendation engine optimized for enjoyment

* A review aggregator (not Goodreads, not IMDB, not Rotten Tomatoes)

* A cinematically curated archive (not MUBI)

* A trending content platform

* A popularity contest

* A tool that tells you what to think

The difference:

| Platform | Curatorial Question |
| :---- | :---- |
| Goodreads | Did you enjoy it? |
| MUBI | Is it cinematically significant? |
| IMDB | Does it exist? |
| **Timeless** | *Will it help someone be human in 100 years?* |

# **3\. THE TEN CATEGORIES**

The ten categories are the editorial policy. They are permanent and non-negotiable.

If a work cannot be assigned to a category with a clear answer to “what human moment does this prepare someone for,” it does not belong in Timeless.

| \# | Category | The Human Moment It Prepares You For |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **1** | **How to Think** | *When your mind is clouded, deceived, or overwhelmed and clarity feels impossible* |
| **2** | **How to Survive** | *When the world breaks you open and you need proof that others endured worse and rebuilt* |
| **3** | **How to Thrive & Build** | *When you’re ready to make something and need models of people who built from nothing* |
| **4** | **How to Love & Be Loved** | *When love is new, failing, lost, or misunderstood and you need to understand it more deeply* |
| **5** | **How to Grieve & Face Loss** | *When someone or something is gone and you don’t know how to carry it* |
| **6** | **How to Know Yourself** | *When you don’t know who you are, where you belong, or what you actually believe* |
| **7** | **How to Be Human & Kind** | *When you’ve forgotten that other people are as complex and fragile as you are* |
| **8** | **How to Lead & Serve** | *When others are depending on you and you don’t know how to hold that weight* |
| **9** | **How to Stay Alive Inside** | *When life feels flat, meaningless, or unlively and you’ve forgotten how to wonder* |
| **10** | **How to Face Power & Injustice** | *When systems are stacked against you and you need to know how to move, survive, or dismantle them* |

# **4\. THE THREE SUPPORTED MEDIUMS**

Timeless accepts works in exactly three mediums. No exceptions.

* Book

* Film

* TV Show

Medium determines how cover art is auto-fetched: Open Library API for books, TMDB for film and TV. Medium is never evaluative. A TV show is not lesser than a book. The question is always the same: does it prepare you for the human moment?

# **5\. THE SUBMISSION STANDARD**

Every submission must clear this bar before it reaches the curator for review.

## **5.1 Required Fields**

* Title

* Author / Director / Creator

* Medium (Book, Film, or TV Show)

* Category (one of the ten)

* Why it belongs here — 2–3 sentences minimum answering: what human moment does this prepare someone for?

* Your name (optional — anonymous attribution permitted)

* Article link (optional — supporting evidence)

## **5.2 The Single Mandatory Question**

The submission form contains one non-optional written field:

***“Why does this belong here? What human moment does it prepare someone for?”***

Minimum: 2–3 sentences. This question is the quality filter. It cannot be answered with “it was entertaining” or “I loved it.” The question self-selects for serious contributors before the submission ever reaches review.

## **5.3 What Passes**

A submission passes if it can answer, with specificity: which category does this belong to, and what human moment does it prepare someone for?

If that question produces a vague or generic answer, the work does not belong in Timeless — regardless of quality, prestige, or popularity.

## **5.4 The Editorial Test**

*Does this teach you how to love well, or just how love feels?*

*Does this prepare you for the human moment, or only describe it?*

Feeling is not instruction. Description is not preparation. The work must equip, not merely evoke.

# **6\. THE APPROVAL PRINCIPLE**

All submissions require curator approval before going live. This is non-negotiable.

The curator’s role is not to impose taste. It is to enforce the standard: does this work prepare someone for a universal human moment, and has the contributor demonstrated they understand why?

The public editorial policy is the ten categories. If a submission cannot be defended against those categories with specificity, it is declined.

## **6.1 What Cannot Be Approved**

* Works submitted without a genuine answer to the mandatory question

* Works whose primary value is entertainment, not preparation

* Works that describe a human experience without equipping the reader or viewer to navigate it

* Duplicate entries without meaningfully distinct contributor perspective

## **6.2 What Must Be Approved**

* Any work that clearly prepares someone for one of the ten human moments

* Any work with a contributor explanation that is specific, honest, and non-generic

* Any work from an underrepresented medium, culture, or perspective that passes the standard

# **7\. THE LANGUAGE PRINCIPLE**

Timeless never declares what is great. It surfaces what lasts.

| Never | Always |
| :---- | :---- |
| "This is a masterpiece" | "This belongs here because..." |
| "You must read this" | "This prepares you for..." |
| "Classic" | "A contributor wrote: ..." |
| "Everyone knows this one" | "Added by someone who needed it when..." |
| "Highly rated" | "Why it lasts: ..." |

The contributor’s voice carries the authority — not the platform’s. Timeless is the frame. The humans are the curators.

# **8\. THE NEWSLETTER PRINCIPLE**

The monthly newsletter is the paid product. The gallery is free.

## **8.1 Structure**

* One book

* One film

* One TV show

* 3–5 of the best community submissions on why each endures

## **8.2 The Sequencing Rule**

The newsletter only works when there is enough quality contributor writing to curate from. The gallery builds the corpus. The newsletter curates it. Do not launch the newsletter until the corpus is real.

## **8.3 Price**

$5–$7 per month. Thoughtful, not premium. Accessible to the person who most needs it.

## **8.4 What It Is Not**

The newsletter is not another recommendation email. It is curated human testimony about what endures — and why. The community writing is the differentiation. Without it, the newsletter is generic.

# **9\. WHAT CAN NEVER CHANGE**

## **9.1 The Curatorial Question**

Every submission, every approval, every newsletter pick must be defensible against one question: will this help someone be human in 100 years? This question is permanent.

## **9.2 The Ten Categories**

The ten categories are the system. They are not genre tags. They are not moods. They are not marketing buckets. They map to universal human crises. They do not change with trends.

## **9.3 Human Curation**

No algorithmic recommendations. No “users who liked this also liked.” The platform is human-curated or it is nothing. This is the non-negotiable differentiator.

## **9.4 The Mandatory Question**

The submission form’s mandatory written field never becomes optional. The moment it becomes optional, quality collapses.

## **9.5 Curator Approval**

Nothing goes live without curator review. Automation does not replace this. Speed does not justify bypassing it.

## **9.6 Medium Neutrality**

A TV show is never treated as lesser than a book. A film is never treated as lesser than a novel. The medium does not determine worth. The preparation does.

# **10\. THE CLOSED LOOP**

Timeless guarantees this loop is never broken:

***Human crisis → Search by category → Find a work → Read why it lasts → Return less alone***

The person arrives broken or deadened or lost. They find a category that names their moment. They read what a real person wrote about why this work helped them survive it. They leave with something to hold.

That is the product. Everything else is infrastructure.

# **11\. FINAL PRINCIPLE**

*Timeless is not a library. It is not a database. It is not a newsletter.*

*It is a letter from one human to another, across time, saying:*

***“Someone made this for the moment you’re in. You are not the first. You will not be the last.”***

*All design, implementation, and evolution must preserve that.*
